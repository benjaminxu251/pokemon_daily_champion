About

Champion Daily

A daily Pokémon battle challenge. Every day everyone gets the same random team and the same opponent: Champion Cynthia under Gen 4 rules. One shot a day; practice as much as you like after.

Inspiration

Champion Daily is inspired by Daily Red Game, a Wordle-style daily battle against Red. Daily Red Game in turn credits amoi-builds' original Daily Red project as its inspiration. Same idea here, with a different champion, Gen 4 rules, and an opponent AI that searches every move and switch with the real battle engine.

How it's built

  • Battles run on @pkmn/sim, the browser build of the Pokémon Showdown simulator (MIT). Your teams come from Showdown's Random Battle sets.
  • The opponents simulate each of their options against each of yours one turn ahead, predict your most likely move, and avoid plays that lose badly.
  • Music and sound effects are original chiptunes; you can swap in your own audio files in Settings.
  • Font: Pixelify Sans (SIL Open Font License).
