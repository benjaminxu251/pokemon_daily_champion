About

Champion Daily

A daily Pokémon battle challenge. Every day everyone gets the same random team and the same opponents: Champion Cynthia under Gen 4 battle rules, Lorekeeper Zinnia under Gen 6 battle rules, and an Elite Four gauntlet drawn from every region under Gen 9 battle rules. One shot a day; practice as much as you like after.

Inspiration

Champion Daily is inspired by Daily Red Game, a Wordle-style daily battle against Red. Daily Red Game in turn credits amoi-builds' original Daily Red project as its inspiration. Same idea here, with different champions, different generations' battle rules, and an opponent AI that searches every move and switch with the real battle engine.

How it's built

  • Battles run on @pkmn/sim, the browser build of the Pokémon Showdown simulator (MIT). Your teams come from Showdown's Random Battle sets.
  • Your Pokémon keep Showdown's Random Battle levels (roughly 65–100). Stronger Pokémon get lower levels, so every day's team is about equally strong. EVs are Showdown's even 85 in every stat.
  • Terastallization is off, so Showdown's Gen 9 "Tera Blast user" sets are skipped. Those Pokémon use their next-best Showdown set instead, for both your team and the Elite Four's.
  • The opponents simulate each of their options against each of yours one turn ahead, predict your most likely move, and avoid plays that lose badly.
  • Pokémon and trainer sprites are linked from Pokémon Showdown's sprite server and belong to Nintendo, Creatures and GAME FREAK; nothing is stored here. Settings can switch to original drawn art. Backgrounds and trainers are original.
  • Music and sound effects are original chiptunes; you can swap in your own audio files in Settings.
  • Font: Pixelify Sans (SIL Open Font License).
